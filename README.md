# YTV

Watch YouTube like TV: your own channels keep playing, full screen, with a swipeable list of what's next. For Chrome and Brave. 100% local: no accounts and no servers.

YTV used to be Hush TV, inside Hush for YouTube. It's now its own extension; Hush keeps tidying YouTube's pages and YTV does the watching. They work well together, and each works on its own.

## Install (Chrome or Brave)

1. Unzip this folder somewhere permanent, such as `Documents/ytv`. The browser loads the extension from it, so don't delete it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` (or `brave://extensions`) and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the `ytv` folder (the one with `manifest.json`).
4. Pin YTV from the puzzle-piece menu.

**Coming from Hush TV?** Update Hush to 5.0 too. Your channels and TV settings come over by themselves the next time you open YouTube. What you've watched, your Feed and your progress carry over as well.

## Using it

- **Start:** the YTV button in YouTube's top bar (with a menu of your channels), the YTV entry in the sidebar, or the toolbar menu.
- **Channels:** your **Feed** (channel 0, your own list), **Home** (the same videos as your YouTube home page, in YouTube's order or shuffled), Subscriptions, **Watch Later** (your whole list and nothing else: it stops at the end), **Twitch Live** (optional: whoever of your streamers is live, in Twitch's own player), and your own channels mixing topics, creators and your home feed.
- **Keys:** **,** and **.** move through the list's tabs (just looking), **Shift+,** and **Shift+.** go to the next / previous tab and play its first video (these take over YouTube's speed keys while YTV is on), **Shift+[** and **Shift+]** go back and forth through everything you watched, across all channels and Twitch, **[** and **]** to the previous / next video, **U** opens the list, **H** switches how the list hides, **`** turns YTV on or off, **E** explores, **Shift+E** searches YouTube (all changeable).
- **Top bar:** rounded blocks (default), one solid bar, or a soft fade into the video, set in Look & feel. YouTube's own title is hidden underneath. Left: the title, with the creator, length and date beneath. Right: back, info, comments and **⋯**, which holds forward, explore, add or hide this creator, Watch Later, and on a stream the quality (with their keys). The channel overview is **G**.
- **Twitch Live:** who's live first (how many watch on each picture; point at one for its ⓘ info button), with who's offline greyed and folded away under one "Offline" bar between them and the live ones (click it to show them); **E** on the Twitch tab shows what's live in a game or category (click a stream to watch it, + to add them); **Shift+E** or the magnifier finds streamers by name. **I** on a stream has a button to add them to Twitch Live or take them off, in your order (drag to change it, or pick "most watched first"). Click a live one to play it; point at one a moment first and it's already playing out of sight, so the click is instant. Nothing in the list moves when you switch. YTV's helper inside Twitch's player (only when YTV shows it on YouTube) gets past the "start watching" and Play buttons by itself, turns the sound on, picks the quality you chose (the gear in the top bar, or settings; Twitch only gives logged-in viewers the higher qualities), keeps a click on the picture from pausing it (**Space** or **K** pauses and plays), and hides Follow, Gift a Sub, Subscribe and "Watch on Twitch". Twitch's own volume slider works as usual; M mutes, ↑ ↓ change the volume, double-click is YTV's full screen, and every YTV key works over the stream too. **I** shows the title, how long they've been live and how many watch, with their **past broadcasts** under it to watch right here (click someone offline for theirs). The chat starts with what was said just before you tuned in. Over the video on dark bars (point at it for its handle: drag it anywhere, resize it from the corner, A− A+ and ◐ for this streamer; there it keeps every message), or in the list panel (the panel goes see-through, the messages keep their dark bars; A− A+ and ◐ in its corner) — one at a time. **O** shows and hides the chat where you keep it (over the video by default), **Shift+O** moves it to the other place; when it shows again it starts with what was said so far. The chat is only connected while you can see it: hide it and the connection closes, show it again and it reconnects with what was just said. Read only, with BetterTTV, FrankerFaceZ and 7TV emotes. Pick the streamers you want a notification about when they go live (also while YouTube is closed). Find streamers by name in settings. After a reload (on a video page or any other YouTube page) the stream comes straight back, and YouTube's own video never plays behind it. (Twitch extensions such as Hearthstone Deck Tracker don't load in Twitch's embedded player, so their pop-ups can't show here.)
- **As a Mac app:** settings → On YouTube → **Set up the app** opens YouTube with YTV's app link and shows what to click (Chrome or Brave). YTV then starts by itself when you open it from the Dock. `https://www.youtube.com/?ytvapp` also works as a bookmark.
- **Full screen:** when YouTube puts its controls up by itself (nobody moved the mouse), YTV and YouTube's controls stay hidden, and YouTube's own full-screen title never shows under YTV's.
- **Info (I):** the description, with the creator, date, length, the channel's subscriber count and the video's views, each with its small icon.
- **History (H, or the clock above the list):** everything you watched, newest first, across channels and Twitch, kept after a restart. Click one to watch it again; what's on now is marked. **Shift+[** and **Shift+]** step through it too. It's a list like the others: swipe one to add it to your Feed (Shift: play next) or forget it.
- **Continue watching** is a channel (right after your Feed until you move it): YouTube videos you started and didn't finish (also ones you half watched on YouTube itself), newest first, each from where you were. It plays like any channel and stops at its end. Not Twitch.
- **Watched:** what you already watched on a channel is folded away under one "Watched" bar right above what's playing; click it to show them (they fade in and out quickly, like Offline on Twitch). Switch the bar off in settings (List → Rows) and they stay in view, greyed out. History keeps the last 200 by default (also there).
- **The list's header:** the funnel, History and refresh (and on Twitch the magnifier) at the top right. Filters that are on show as one pill ("2 filters · Clear").
- **Feedback** stays on the row: a short flash when something worked, a small shake when that way isn't possible.
- **Settings** open right over TV (from the channel overview), so full screen stays as it is; ✕ or Esc closes them.
- **Hiding:** the setting in List (a key for it can be set under Keyboard) switches between hiding the list whenever the mouse rests, only in full screen, or never. Point at the right edge of the screen (between the top bar and YouTube's controls) and the list slides out, and the small tab just outside its left edge closes it again; it goes again a moment after the mouse is back over the video (not when you overshoot to the browser's tabs).
- **Icons:** Feed and the ready-made channels show as icons in the tab bar, and small icons sit before the creator, date and length (list and top bar each switchable). The title has its own size; the rest of its line is a little smaller.
- **The list (U):** its tabs follow your channel order (as in the overview, G; dragging a tab moves the channel). **H**, **I** and **O** open History, Info or Comments; pressed again they take you back to the list (if you came from it) or close the panel (if you were just watching). When the panel hides by itself, it comes back as the list. **S** switches sorting off and on: off, the list stays exactly as it is however much you switch (nothing greys, folds, moves or goes, and it doesn't scroll to what's playing); back on, it counts from that moment: fully watched videos (90% by default, set under List → Rows) leave the tab you're on (Cmd+Z brings them back), and what's playing moves to the top with everything else in its order after it. Each tab remembers where you scrolled; the first time, what's playing sits at the top with what you watched just above it, out of view. It fades away together with YouTube's player controls when the mouse rests (switchable), never while you point at it. Compact tabs that stay where they are: a new one opens on the right, drag one sideways to move it, a middle click closes it, and/or an ✕ on each tab (both in settings → List → Tabs), and **+** adds a channel as a tab. The ▾ tab for looking at any channel is optional (List & swiping): Feed · channels you pinned · the channel playing · the channel you're looking at (▾ picks any) · topics you're exploring · **+** pins a channel as a tab.
- **Explore (E):** pick a topic from the video, or Cmd-click several to explore them together (or type them with commas). Hashtags find their videos too. Typing there never reaches the video, and the video behind is softly blurred.
- **Search (Shift+E):** search YouTube for videos; the results open as a tab in the list, where you swipe one to add it to your Feed or click one to watch it.
- **Several topics at once:** exploring a second topic keeps the first as its own tab with what was left of it. Pick a video in a waiting topic to carry on with that one.
- **Filter (the funnel above the list, next to ↻):** length (under / over X minutes, or both for a range), uploaded (within / older than X days, weeks or months), watched (yes / no, from X% watched; 80% by default) and subscribed (yes / no). One set for every YouTube tab, kept after a reload and used for everything that loads later; autoplay skips what's filtered out. The filters that are on show as chips under the tabs (× turns one off). Never your Feed or Twitch, and what's playing always stays.
- **Swiping:** two fingers sideways on a video. One side removes it, the other adds it to your Feed (and out of this list). Hold **Shift** while you swipe to the plus side (its own colour, set under Swiping) and the video plays next instead, also from another tab; if it's already further down the list it moves up, never twice. On the keyboard, point at a video and press **A** (add), **Shift+A** (play next) or **D** (remove). A way a row can't go (removing what's playing, say) gives a little, glides back once and says why. A short stop in the middle keeps you from swiping straight through from one side to the other. **Cmd/Ctrl+Z** undoes the last change, a move as a whole.
- **What's playing** is clearly red. Once its row scrolls away over the top, a slim copy slides in over the list in step with your scrolling; click it to jump back.
- **Reorder** by holding a video and moving it: a copy follows the pointer smoothly and the list scrolls near the edges.
- **The clock on a picture** saves to Watch Later, and takes it off again if it's already there.
- **Load more:** a button at the end of a list, and it's endless: scroll near the end and the next page of every source loads by itself (and related videos when a small creator runs out); the button still works too. Far down a list, an arrow takes you back to the top, and the list fades softly at its edges.
- **Pick several:** shift-click a range (Cmd/Ctrl-click one at a time), then explore them together as their own topic, add them all to your Feed, or remove them all. One Cmd+Z undoes it; Esc lets go.
- **Your Feed remembers where videos came from:** take one off unwatched and it goes back to its channel, where it was.
- **Keys and clicks** bring the hidden UI back and keep it up for a moment.
- **Removing** a video: it fades where it is, then the rows below move up into its place (300 ms, adjustable).
- **Your Feed** videos are in no other list: not on Home, Watched, Watch Later, your channels or search results. Swiping the video that's playing adds it to your Feed and it stays where it is.
- **Hidden creators** are gone from every channel by default (switchable in Your channels); YTV remembers which @handle, channel id and name belong together, so hiding one name hides them all.
- **Order:** watched videos (a short trail, 3 by default) → what's playing → what's next. New videos always go after what's playing.
- **Opens where you left it:** turn YTV off (or close the tab) and on again and it carries on with the same video, from the same moment, with the same list after it and the same panel open, straight away.
- **Carries on where you left off:** go back to a video and it continues from where you were, also after watching a stream in between, after a reload, or when you pick it in another channel's tab.
- **Channel overview (G):** four layouts to pick in settings: a small poster wall (what's on now as a picture, what's next as a strip), classic cards (a picture of what's on now and its title), a TV guide (one line per channel with the next three side by side) or big pictures. Each card has a **+** to pin a channel as a tab.
- Drag any video to reorder it; Delete (or D) removes the one under the pointer, A adds it to your Feed, Shift+A plays it next; hover a picture to explore it or save it to Watch Later.

## Settings

Every block has **Reset to default**, settings you changed are marked, and the preview next to the page (list, top bar, colours, overview and Twitch pages) follows every change while you drag, lighting up the part of the screen the block you point at changes. Sizes have no small maximums any more (text, tabs, rounding, blur, chat and so on go as large as you like).

- **Channels:** *Your channels* (topics, creators with YouTube search and "Similar", hidden creators, filters; each folds open), *Ready-made channels* (Home and its order, Subscriptions, Watch Later) and *Twitch* (Twitch Live on / off, find and add streamers, order, quality, offline streamers, where O puts the chat, the chat over the video and in the panel, emotes, go-live notifications).
- **Watching:** *List* (when it hides, the right edge, tabs: middle-click and ✕ each on their own, rows, row text, the buttons on a picture), *Swiping* (a live demo, direction, distance, colours, the keyboard keys), *Top bar*, *Colours & style* (themes, colours, list panel, size, animations), *Channel overview*, *Playing* (skip music, buttons above the player bar) and *Keyboard* (your keys, plus the fixed ones).
- **YTV:** *On YouTube* (top bar and sidebar buttons), a search box at the top (Cmd/Ctrl+F) that finds any setting, *Health check* (tries everything YTV reads from YouTube and makes a report to send when something breaks), *Backup* (export / import, also takes a Hush settings file) and *About*.

## Privacy and security

- Permissions: only `storage`, and only on `www.youtube.com`, plus Twitch's player (`player.twitch.tv`) for the Twitch Live helper, which does nothing unless YouTube embeds the player. Twitch Live asks your browser for `gql.twitch.tv` only when you switch it on; YTV then only asks Twitch which of your streamers are live (no cookies sent). The chat over a stream is an anonymous, read-only connection to Twitch's chat (nothing can be sent from it), and the extra emotes come from the public lists of BetterTTV, FrankerFaceZ and 7TV.
- Apart from that, YTV only talks to youtube.com, through two functions in `src/yt-data.js` that refuse any other address: reading YouTube pages, and YouTube's own API for comments, the next page of a list, and your Watch Later list. Your Watch Later list only changes when you ask.
- No remote or dynamic code, no `innerHTML`, no analytics. Messages are only accepted from YTV itself.

## Developing

YTV runs as one file, `src/ytv.js`, but is edited as parts in `dev/tv/` (`01-data.js` … `07-start.js`, plus `tv.css`). After changing a part, run `node dev/build.js`. Never edit `src/ytv.js` by hand.

`tests/tv/` has browser tests on a mock YouTube page (Node, Playwright and Chromium): start `python3 tests/tv/serve.py`, then `node tests/tv/run.js` (YTV itself) and `node tests/tv/pages.js` (settings and toolbar menu) and `node tests/tv/soak.js` (opens and closes YTV many times and checks nothing piles up). `node tests/tv/stress.js` is heavy use (a chat flooding at 300 messages a second, 30 stream switches, YTV opened and closed on Twitch, a list of hundreds with Load more): smooth, nothing left behind, memory flat. `node tests/tv/e2e-twitch.js` loads the real extension with stand-ins for YouTube, Twitch's player and Twitch's chat (it needs a self-signed certificate for irc-ws.chat.twitch.tv in `E2E_DIR`).
